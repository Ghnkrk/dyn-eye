The 5 pillars that will make you genuinely stand out
Advanced RAG — retrieval quality, reranking, citations, Graph RAG, agentic workflows.
LLM Evaluation — automated benchmarks, faithfulness, regression testing, LLM-as-a-judge.
AI Agents — planning, memory, orchestration, tool use, recovery from failures.
Inference & Deployment — serving models efficiently and understanding performance trade-offs.
AI System Design — architecting production-ready LLM applications end to end.

Starting with Advanced RAG is an excellent choice because it naturally ties together retrieval, embeddings, vector search, evaluation, agents, and system design. Once you understand industry-grade RAG, many of the other topics become much easier to contextualize.
